# Requirements of the staircase lighting installation

Example document of the bcdd-editor guide: the requirements that the CSCs of the folder cite.

<a id="REQ-1"></a>**REQ-1** A press on any button switches the light on within 1 s.

<a id="REQ-2"></a>**REQ-2** The light stays on for 90 s after the last press, with a tolerance of 5 s; then it switches off by itself.

<a id="REQ-3"></a>**REQ-3** The installation draws at most 100 W from the mains of the block of flats.
